BBQ Chicken Pizza

Description

This BBQ chicken pizza recipe is a delicious and easy-to-make pizza that combines the smoky flavors of barbecue sauce with tender chicken, red onions, and melted cheese. The pizza crust is crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, providing the perfect base for the flavorful toppings. This pizza is perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or a weekend gathering with friends and family.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
  • ½ cup barbecue sauce
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded
  • ½ cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 ½ cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh cilantro leaves, for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If you have a pizza stone, place it in the oven to heat up while you prepare the pizza.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a lightly floured surface to your desired thickness. Transfer the rolled-out dough to a piece of parchment paper for easy handling.
  3. Spread the barbecue sauce evenly over the surface of the dough, leaving a small border around the edges for the crust.
  4. Top the sauce with shredded chicken, sliced red onions, and shredded mozzarella cheese.
  5. If using a pizza stone, carefully transfer the pizza (with the parchment paper) onto the preheated stone. If not using a stone, place the pizza on a baking sheet and bake in the preheated oven.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and crispy and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  7. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing. Garnish with fresh cilantro leaves if desired. Serve hot and enjoy!
